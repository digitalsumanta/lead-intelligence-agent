### 4. Business Impact of Identified Issues

1. **Diluted Search Engine Crawling & Semantic Indexing:**
   - *Technical Cause:* The HTML `<h1>` element provides search engines with the primary thematic signal of a page. Its absence forces crawlers (Googlebot) to infer the page's core topic from secondary elements or body text.
   - *Business Impact:* Reduced ability to rank competitively for high-intent generic residential search terms in South Kolkata (e.g., "luxury apartment interior designers Hazra", "modular kitchen specialists Kalighat") despite having strong local brand signals.
2. **Missed High-Intent Organic Traffic & Top-of-Funnel Capture:**
   - *Technical Cause:* Without an editorial content hub, the website only ranks for navigational and direct branded searches.
   - *Business Impact:* Prospective homeowners in Kolkata researching planning advice, material selection (e.g., acrylic vs. PU finish, veneer maintenance, space-saving layouts for 2BHK/3BHKs) land on competitor portals or design aggregators rather than discovering INAAYA INTERIOR.
3. **Sub-Optimized Yield on Paid Acquisition Investment:**
   - *Technical Cause:* The presence of a Google Ads Tag indicates paid acquisition investment, yet landing page flows direct paid traffic into generic brochure pages rather than specialized, conversion-focused landing experiences.
   - *Business Impact:* Higher effective cost-per-inquiry and missed attribution intelligence on which creative assets generate high-ticket residential consultations.

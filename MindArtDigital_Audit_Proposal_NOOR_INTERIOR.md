### 4. Business Impact of Identified Issues

1. **Suppressed Click-Through Rates (CTR) in Organic Search:**
   - *Technical Cause:* When an HTML meta description is absent, search engines dynamically construct snippet descriptions from whatever text fragments appear near the top of the webpage (often navigation text, cookie banners, or disjointed headings).
   - *Business Impact:* Prospective commercial and residential clients searching for "turnkey interior designers Kolkata" or "modular kitchen designers VIP Nagar" see unappealing, truncated search snippets, significantly reducing organic click-through rates compared to competitors with professionally curated meta descriptions.
2. **Under-Leveraged 4.8★ Local Reputation:**
   - *Technical Cause:* The company’s impressive 167 reviews are confined to Google Maps and are not semantically linked into the website through structured review schema markup.
   - *Business Impact:* Organic search listings miss out on displaying rich star rating snippets in standard SERPs, sacrificing immediate trust signals before the prospective client even clicks through.
3. **Missed Commercial and Office Fit-Out Lead Capture:**
   - *Technical Cause:* The current website acts as a static brochure without dedicated service pages for corporate interiors or commercial fit-outs.
   - *Business Impact:* Commercial decision-makers (office managers, retail owners) evaluating turnkey design partners cannot readily evaluate previous office execution scopes, resulting in lost qualified B2B inquiries.
